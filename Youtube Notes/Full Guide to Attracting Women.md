# Self-Improvement & Dating

This guide outlines a holistic approach to becoming more attractive by improving your health, appearance, communication skills, and relationship habits. The emphasis is on long-term self-improvement rather than relying on quick fixes.

## 1. Looks-Maxing (The Foundation)

### Build an Aesthetic Physique *(4:34 – 10:44)*

- Follow a consistent strength-training program.
- Focus on building a balanced physique, especially:
  - Shoulders (lateral delts).
  - Upper chest.
  - Back.
  - Arms.
- Maintain good posture and overall athleticism.

### Facial Appearance

#### Tongue Posture ("Mewing") *(12:50 – 13:13)*

- Maintain healthy tongue posture by resting your tongue against the roof of your mouth.
- Evidence for significant facial changes in adults is limited, but good oral posture may support normal nasal breathing and posture.

#### Nasal Breathing *(14:23 – 17:36)*

- Prioritize breathing through your nose whenever possible, especially during sleep if medically appropriate.
- If you experience chronic mouth breathing or suspected sleep-disordered breathing, consult a healthcare professional before trying interventions such as mouth taping.

#### Maintain a Healthy Body Fat Percentage *(17:38)*

- Lower body fat can make facial features appear more defined.
- Aim for a healthy, sustainable body composition rather than extreme leanness.

### Grooming *(22:40 – 23:44)*

- Keep your appearance clean and well-maintained.
- Prioritize:
  - A hairstyle that suits your face.
  - Good skincare.
  - Proper hygiene.
  - Well-kept facial hair, if you choose to have it.

## 2. Meeting Women

### Dating Apps *(57:15 – 59:55)*

- Use clear, high-quality photos.
- Show your lifestyle and personality.
- Keep opening messages simple and natural.
- If the conversation is flowing, suggest meeting in person rather than texting indefinitely.

### Meeting People in Real Life *(1:01:13 – 1:11:07)*

- Be open to conversations during everyday activities.
- Start with:
  - Genuine compliments.
  - Contextual observations.
  - Simple questions.
- Prioritize authenticity over memorized pickup lines.

---

## 3. Conversation & Attraction

### Listen More *(43:35 – 45:21)*

- Encourage the other person to share about themselves.
- Practice active listening.
- Ask thoughtful follow-up questions.
- Avoid dominating the conversation.

### Confident Body Language *(52:11 – 53:24)*

- Stand with relaxed posture.
- Maintain comfortable eye contact.
- Speak at a calm pace.
- Let physical affection develop naturally through mutual comfort and consent.

---

## 4. Building Healthy Relationships

### Avoid Complacency *(121:25 – 128:00)*

- Continue investing in yourself after entering a relationship.
- Maintain:
  - Your health.
  - Your appearance.
  - Your hobbies.
  - Your ambitions.
- Personal growth benefits both you and the relationship.

### Understand Attachment Styles *(132:19 – 143:31)*

- Learn about your attachment patterns.
- Practice secure relationship behaviors such as:
  - Honest communication.
  - Emotional consistency.
  - Trust.
  - Healthy boundaries.

### Respond to Bids for Connection *(149:25 – 151:28)*

- Notice small moments when your partner seeks attention or connection.
- Responding positively to these moments helps strengthen intimacy and long-term relationship satisfaction.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Neglecting physical health.
- Depending entirely on dating apps.
- Talking more than listening.
- Neglecting personal growth after entering a relationship.
- Ignoring emotional communication.
- Expecting appearance alone to create lasting attraction.

---

## Core Principle

> Lasting attractiveness comes from becoming healthier, more confident, socially skilled, and emotionally mature. Physical appearance may create initial interest, but meaningful relationships are built through authenticity, communication, mutual respect, and continuous self-improvement.
