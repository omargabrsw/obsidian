# A Masculine Approach to Texting

This guide presents a texting philosophy centered on authenticity, confidence, and maintaining a fulfilling life outside of dating. The goal is to use texting as a way to support real-world connections rather than replace them.

## 1. Core Principles

### Avoid Overthinking *(2:33 – 3:45)*

- Don't obsess over:
  - Reply times.
  - Read receipts.
  - Snapchat streaks.
  - Texting "rules."
- Focus on having genuine conversations instead of playing mind games.

### Be Authentic *(4:19 – 4:42)*

- Text because you genuinely want to connect.
- Avoid sending messages purely to:
  - Seek validation.
  - Get attention.
  - Follow scripted advice.
- Authenticity is more sustainable than trying to perform a role.

### Focus on Real-Life Connection *(10:59 – 11:31)*

- Texting should support meeting in person, not replace it.
- Prioritize real experiences over endless digital conversations.

---

## 2. Actionable Strategies

### Keep Texts Simple *(5:39 – 8:05)*

- Send natural, low-pressure messages.
- Mention:
  - A shared experience.
  - Something that reminded you of them.
  - An observation from your day.
- Avoid forcing compliments or overcomplicated conversations.

### Build an Interesting Life *(13:46 – 15:10)*

- Invest in yourself first.
- Focus on:
  - Fitness.
  - Hobbies.
  - Friendships.
  - Learning.
  - New experiences.
- A fulfilling lifestyle naturally gives you more to share and makes dating a complement to your life rather than its center.

### Invite, Don't Chase *(19:24 – 19:52)*

- Make plans because they genuinely interest you.
- If appropriate, invite the other person to join.
- This communicates initiative while respecting their freedom to accept or decline.

---

## 3. Know When to Move On

### Respect Rejection *(15:12 – 16:51)*

- If someone repeatedly declines invitations or continually cancels without suggesting another time, take the hint.
- Avoid repeatedly pursuing someone who isn't showing reciprocal interest.
- Invest your energy where enthusiasm is mutual.

### Value Your Time *(16:03 – 16:30)*

- Don't make your value depend on constant texting.
- Your attention and time are limited resources.
- Healthy relationships involve mutual effort and interest.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Overanalyzing every message.
- Playing texting games.
- Seeking validation through constant communication.
- Letting texting replace real-life interaction.
- Continuing to pursue someone who consistently shows little interest.

---

## Core Principle

> Texting works best when it reflects a confident, fulfilling life. Be authentic, keep communication simple, prioritize real-world experiences, and invest your time in relationships where interest and effort are mutual.
