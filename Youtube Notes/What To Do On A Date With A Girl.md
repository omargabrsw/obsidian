# What To Do on a Date

This guide outlines principles for becoming more confident and intentional in dating by developing yourself, communicating well, and respecting mutual interest and boundaries.

## 1. Prerequisites *(2:08 – 6:06)*

### Physical Fitness *(2:16)*

- Build and maintain a consistent strength-training routine.
- Prioritize:
  - Physical health.
  - Fitness.
  - Grooming.
- Looking after your body improves both confidence and attractiveness.

### Lifestyle *(5:09)*

- Become an interesting person outside of dating.
- Regularly pursue:
  - New experiences.
  - Hobbies.
  - Social events.
  - Adventures.
- A fulfilling life gives you stories, confidence, and genuine enthusiasm to share.

---

## 2. Date Dynamics *(7:55 – 24:05)*

### Leadership

- Take initiative throughout the date.
- Examples include:
  - Choosing the venue.
  - Leading transitions.
  - Making decisions confidently.
- Maintain open, relaxed body language.
- Avoid nervous habits such as:
  - Fidgeting.
  - Touching your face excessively.
  - Closed-off posture.

### Eye Contact *(12:26)*

- Hold comfortable, natural eye contact.
- Use it to communicate:
  - Confidence.
  - Interest.
  - Presence.
- Avoid forcing intensity or staring.

### The 80/20 Rule *(21:00 – 23:42)*

- Encourage her to speak more than you.
- Listen actively.
- Ask thoughtful follow-up questions.
- Keep your responses concise enough to encourage deeper conversation.

### Social Confidence *(27:46)*

- Be friendly and respectful toward everyone.
- Confidently interact with:
  - Waitstaff.
  - Service workers.
  - People around you.
- Good social skills demonstrate confidence and emotional maturity.

---

## 3. Building Physical Intimacy *(36:43 – 46:26)*

### Gradual Progression *(37:32)*

- Let physical affection develop naturally.
- Start with small, appropriate gestures.
- Pay attention to comfort and reciprocal interest.
- Respect personal boundaries at every stage.

### The First Kiss *(39:46 – 40:15)*

- If the moment feels mutually welcome, approach calmly and confidently.
- If the interest isn't shared:
  - Accept it respectfully.
  - Stay relaxed.
  - Don't become defensive.
  - Continue the interaction naturally if appropriate.

### Intentionality *(42:07 – 45:10)*

- Be honest about your intentions.
- If both people want to continue spending time together, communicate clearly.
- Let mutual enthusiasm and consent guide every decision.

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Being overly nervous.
- Trying too hard to impress.
- Talking more than listening.
- Constantly seeking validation.
- Ignoring body language or boundaries.
- Neglecting self-improvement outside of dating.

---

## Core Principle

> Attraction is built on confidence, an interesting life, strong communication, emotional presence, and respect for mutual interest and boundaries. The best dates feel natural because both people are comfortable, engaged, and enjoying the experience.
