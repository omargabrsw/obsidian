# How to Become an Influential Self-Improvement Coach

This guide outlines the principles, coaching structure, and tools needed to effectively coach friends toward self-improvement.

## 1. Becoming Influential

### Lead the Way *(2:57 – 6:37)*

- Practice what you preach.
- Build a **portfolio of results** to establish credibility.
  - Body transformation photos.
  - Meditation app streaks.
  - Other measurable personal achievements.
- Influence comes from demonstrated success, not just advice.

### Education & Humility *(7:08 – 12:28)*

- Treat coaching as a learnable skill.
- Maintain a **lifelong student mindset**.
- Keep your ego low.
- Continuously study topics such as:
  - Listening skills.
  - Negotiation.
  - Influence.
  - Human psychology.

### Sacrifice Previous Priorities *(15:28 – 17:10)*

- Let go of habits and values that conflict with your new goals.
- Increased focus requires intentional sacrifice.

### Discuss the Undiscussible *(17:12 – 18:55)*

- Be willing to discuss difficult or taboo topics.
- Honest conversations build:
  - Trust.
  - Authenticity.
  - Deeper relationships.

---

## 2. The Three-Step Coaching Structure

### Step 1: Initial Conversation *(19:11 – 27:30)*

- Schedule a video call.
- Understand:
  - Their goals.
  - Current struggles.
  - Lifestyle.
  - Mental health baseline.
- Use the **NHS Mental Health Test** as a starting assessment.

### Step 2: Make a Plan *(28:32 – 40:47)*

- Create a personalized habit tracker.
- Use **client journaling** to:
  - Understand their perspective.
  - Anticipate limiting beliefs.
  - Design better interventions.
- Recommend impactful books such as:
  - *The Magic of Thinking Big*.

### Step 3: Daily Accountability *(41:00 – 58:00)*

- Hold short daily calls or send check-in messages.
- Guide rather than command.
- Use thought-provoking questions instead of giving orders.

**Example**

Instead of:

> "Go work out."

Ask:

> "Do you think you'd feel better if you worked out today?"

This helps clients feel ownership over their habits.
