## 🕋 The Master Summer Time Table

| Time Block | Phase | Action Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **04:30 AM – 05:30 AM** | **Fajr & Power Hour** | Wake up, **1L water**, Pray Fajr, Sunlight. Open Obsidian for your **Daily Audit + 5-Min Meditation** on the mat. No scrolling. |
| **05:30 AM – 09:30 AM** | **Deep Work Block** | 4 Hours of pure Programming or Business Building. Phone completely out of the room. |
| **09:30 AM – 10:30 AM** | **The First Fuel & Read** | High-protein/high-fat meal. Spend **15–20 minutes reading** a physical book or educational material while digesting. *(Optional: 20-min tactical nap here if sleep felt short).* |
| **10:30 AM – 12:30 PM** | **Content Creation & Life Tasks** | **Your Creator Block:** Scripting videos, editing, or researching hooks. Use Saturdays for general life admin tasks. |
| **12:30 PM – 01:00 PM** | **Dhuhr & Prep** | Pray Dhuhr. Hydrate and mentally lock into training mode. |
| **01:00 PM – 03:00 PM** | **The Battle Block** | **30 min:** Mobility Playlist (Warm-up/Flexibility) <br> **45–60 min:** High-intensity Calisthenics to failure on rings/bars. <br> **Alternating Days:** Hit your 10 rounds of shadowboxing for fat loss. |
| **03:00 PM – 04:30 PM** | **Recovery & Digest** | Cold shower, change, rest, and keep hydrated. Let your body cool down and your nervous system reset. |
| **04:30 PM – 05:00 PM** | **Asr & Main Fuel** | Pray Asr. Eat your main massive meal of the day to rebuild your muscles now that blood flow has returned to your gut. |
| **05:00 PM – 08:30 PM** | **Monetization & Social XP** | High-productivity window. Review code, finish video edits, or **leave the house**. Go to a cafe, look people in the eye (+20 XP), and work where the world can see you. Stop hiding. |
| **08:30 PM – 09:30 PM** | **Maghrib & Twilight Wind-Down** | Pray Maghrib. Drop all high stimulation and bright screens. Read, do your **Obsidian Self-Correction Audit**, and slow down your brain. |
| **09:30 PM – 10:00 PM** | **Isha & Night Shutdown** | Pray Isha. Set your 1L water bottle on your desk for tomorrow. Write down tomorrow's exact coding objectives. |
| **10:00 PM** | **Sleep** | Lights out immediately. Full physical and mental recovery. |
